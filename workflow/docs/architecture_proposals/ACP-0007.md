## Current invariant or soft constraint

ACP-0006 §17 states:

> The first integrated path uses fixed topology and must run with structural
> plasticity disabled. No N3 edge adaptation or new candidate-evidence
> semantics are activated by integration. Static topology and its bounded
> Model-B parameters remain available. Structural adaptation may be
> reintroduced only under its existing local evidence contract and separate
> authorization; this proposal does not redesign it.

A14 supports bounded local structural adaptation. This draft considers an
optional, explicitly selected EXCURSION_V1 experiment path; it does not
propose making structural plasticity a default or mandatory core behavior.
ACP-0002 N2 remains unchanged. ACP-0002 N3 remains unauthorized. No neuron
equation, edge parameter (`w`, `d`, or `r`), prediction/error arithmetic,
readout algorithm, or hardware-equivalence claim is proposed.

## Observed problem

At baseline `d9abe5e3a5b9b3c6d6049ac4c64647463c33ba2d`, `ExperimentConfig`
rejects `EXCURSION_V1` with `structural_plasticity=True`. This preserves
ACP-0006's first-integration fixed-topology boundary. The historical
`ExperimentRunner._adapt_topology()` is not a conforming EXCURSION_V1 evidence
producer: it selects a source by example index, selects destinations from a
configured policy, scores with `abs(run.feature) + run.loss`, and prunes using
feature-derived scores. The score inputs are post-character experiment
summaries, not evidence shown to be locally available to the candidate source.

The Luna-12I `TemporalAssociationPolicy` is a bounded experimental policy
that emits the existing `CandidateEvidence` type. Luna-13F independently
supports runtime-generated source-local evidence in its declared fixture and
supports that the evidence affects canonical admission. Luna-13F does **not**
support useful-growth prediction, does not establish resource benefit, and
does not authorize use in normal EXCURSION_V1 training. Its runtime fixture
uses legacy `TPCNNeuron` and explicitly addressed candidate observations; it
does not define how a production E2 source obtains candidate observations from
the actual routed event path.

Luna-13D did not establish evidence-based pruning: its scores were assigned
by endpoint role, and changing the declared utility threshold did not change
the pruning decisions. Luna-13E concerns pre-admission candidate quality after
fixture pruning; it does not supply a local edge-retention/pruning evidence
contract.

## Proposed change

This draft asks the project owner to decide whether to authorize a separately
bounded EXCURSION_V1 structural experiment. The recommended first step is
**growth-only**, explicitly opt-in, and remains an experiment rather than
production-default behavior.

Recommended constraints, pending owner decision:

1. Keep the existing EXCURSION_V1 structural-mode guard until an accepted
   contract and a separate implementation dispatch exist.
2. Permit decisions only at a quiescent boundary after a complete character
   has settled and its runtime has destroyed its queue, route sidecar, pending
   internal work, prediction/error traffic, and character-local ledgers. Never
   mutate during an active character. This selects the post-character,
   pre-next-`START_CHARACTER` boundary for the proposal, rather than a
   workload/epoch or training-phase boundary.
3. Permit growth only in the first experiment. Keep pruning disabled until a
   source-local edge-retention/pruning signal and its evidence chronology are
   separately specified and tested. Preserve the historical rule that
   already queued routed events survive pruning.
4. Continue using the single `BoundedTopology` instance for admission and
   subsequent E2 routing. Preserve bounded admission, finite positive delays,
   deterministic ties, and all-or-none topology mutation.
5. Create bounded evidence state per character, freeze it only after a fully
   completed character, consume that frozen candidate set once at the
   quiescent boundary, and discard all evidence/candidates before the next
   character. Do not carry structural evidence across characters, epochs, or
   experiment/model resets. Persistent topology remains governed by ACP-0006.
   Incomplete/budget-exhausted characters contribute no structural evidence.
6. Keep fixed-topology EXCURSION_V1 as an explicit matched control. A
   `TANH_LEGACY` run may be retained as a separately tagged compatibility
   comparison where meaningful; numeric equality is not required.
7. Do not require prediction-loss or accuracy improvement for mechanism
   acceptance. Report task, prediction, events, route depth, queue, mutation,
   proxy-energy, and readout observations without treating them as admission
   inputs. Luna-13F remains a negative result for useful-growth prediction.

The owner decision must additionally specify the actual E2-local observation
surface that may generate a candidate; how observed event types and source/
destination identities map to `observer`, `source`, and `destination`; the
score and delay provenance; the behavior for incomplete settling; and the
evidence freeze/consume/reset lifecycle. This draft does not silently select
the experimental fixture adapter as a production event producer.

## Why implementation alone cannot solve it

The generic `CandidateEvidence` record validates types, finite score and
delay, endpoint membership, and `observer == source`; it cannot validate
where its score came from. The controller therefore cannot turn
`run.feature + run.loss` into source-local evidence by setting `observer` to
the chosen source. The E2 runtime currently provides actual per-character
routing and a quiescent destruction boundary, but no accepted structural
evidence adapter or cross-character evidence lifecycle. Selecting those
semantics would promote an experimental producer and define new integrated
behavior, not merely wire an already-complete producer.

## Expected benefits

An accepted decision could permit a bounded test of whether a legal,
source-local EXCURSION_V1 topology mutation changes a later real routed
excursion. It would clarify a root dependency behind several blocked
structural/replay consumers while keeping structural efficacy separate from
mechanism validity.

## Expected disadvantages and risks

Local temporal association did not predict useful held-out growth in Luna-13F.
Growth may increase events, proxy energy, route depth, or harmful connectivity
without improving a task. A source may not locally observe the activity of a
potential destination in a unidirectional network; importing a central runtime
log would violate A07. Character-order learning can make later examples see a
different topology. Growth-only mode may fill finite capacity without a
validated retention policy. None of these risks is solved by removing the
configuration guard.

## Hardware implications

No hardware result is claimed. Any accepted evidence feed must use bounded
local state and finite delays and must not require a global neural clock,
unbounded event log, global topology search, or visualization dependency.
Software timestamps remain logical reference values until hardware units and
mapping are separately declared. No FPGA, FPAA, hybrid, or backend equivalence
is established.

## Learning and energy implications

Labels, held-out results, class prototypes, global accuracy, confusion
matrices, outer readout features, task rewards, and postprocessed prediction
loss must not become structural evidence unless a separate accepted contract
explicitly authorizes a particular local message and timing boundary.
Prediction/error, eligibility, delayed credit, and outer reward arithmetic
remain unchanged. Energy remains an uncalibrated activity-cost proxy; the
topology must not be selected solely to minimize that proxy.

## Compatibility and migration

The current guard remains until this proposal is accepted and a bounded Luna
dispatch is separately approved. Existing TANH_LEGACY behavior and the
Luna-12E fixed-topology path remain available. TPCV-1/TPCV-2 remain
downstream-only. IR-2 is not extended and is not a whole-network structural
checkpoint. Existing spiral, Luna-12L, temporal-analysis, and viewer
consumers require separate migration/review and are not implementation
dependencies of the proposed first experiment.

## Required experiments and acceptance criteria

Before any implementation authorization, resolve the open decisions above.
If authorized, the owning dispatch should require, at minimum:

- explicit fixed-topology E2 control, deterministic structural repeat, and a
  TANH_LEGACY compatibility comparison only where semantically appropriate;
- source-local evidence provenance from actual E2 runtime events, score
  chronology, freeze behavior, label mutation, and future-suffix controls;
- no mutation while the character runtime is active and no evidence from
  incomplete/budget-exhausted characters;
- finite candidate/evidence history, mutation budget, topology, fan-in/out,
  routing capacity, and positive finite delay; deterministic capacity
  rejection and no partial topology mutation;
- at least one legal bounded candidate/admission route to useful convergent
  fan-in remains measurable under the declared capacity budget; capacity is
  not treated as an optimization target;
- capture-on/off equivalence and same-topology structure/computation;
- at least one admitted growth case that changes a later real delayed E2
  routed-excursion trace; topology existence alone is insufficient;
- separate reporting of mechanism status and task efficacy, with no required
  accuracy/prediction-loss improvement threshold;
- no pruning until a distinct local edge-use/retention evidence contract and
  a later-route-removal causal test are approved.

No Luna-13F useful-growth claim, A14 promotion, N3 work, hardware acceptance,
or downstream-consumer migration follows from passing this mechanism gate.

## Rollback plan

No code or accepted contract is changed by this draft. The known-good state is
fixed-topology EXCURSION_V1 under ACP-0006 §17 at baseline
`d9abe5e3a5b9b3c6d6049ac4c64647463c33ba2d`. Rejecting or superseding this
proposal leaves the current guard and topology unchanged.
