architecture document. It freezes schema revision 1 with trial/window,

window is required to infer silence; truncated/censored trials are explicit.

for a separately authorized task contract, not chosen here.

missed positive FN; complete negative silence TN; emitting negative FP;

cannot rescue it or receive new reward/penalty semantics. Ambiguous-prefix
and duplicate diagnostics do not inflate confusion-matrix denominators.
