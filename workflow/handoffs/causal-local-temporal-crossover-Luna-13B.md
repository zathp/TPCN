## Frozen design

The implemented score is:

`score = abs(local_residual) * exp(-scoring_decay * elapsed)`

The local residual is generated by the canonical neuron from a numeric event
delivered through `EventQueue` and `BoundedTopology`:

`local_residual = amplitude * exp(-execution_decay * interval)`

The frozen configuration uses amplitudes `(0.7, 1.0)`, intervals `(1.0, 3.0)`,
execution decay `0.1`, scoring-decay conditions `0.0` and `0.2`, one available
structural edge slot, event budget `12`, queue capacity `16`, and candidate
propagation delay `1.0`.

The analytic equality is:

`r_left * exp(-mu * dt_left) = r_right * exp(-mu * dt_right)`

with residuals `0.6333861926251716` and `0.7408182206817179`, interval delta
`2.0`, and frozen crossover `mu* = 0.07833747196936626`. This derivation and
configuration were fixed before the held-out low/high comparison.

## Observed

- Low scoring decay (`0.0`): scores left/right = `0.6333861926251716` /
  `0.7408182206817179`; rank `right,left`; admitted edge `right -> target`.
- High scoring decay (`0.2`): scores left/right = `0.5185727544772024` /
  `0.4065696597405991`; rank `left,right`; admitted edge `left -> target`.
- Both primary runs had exactly two candidates, one available edge slot, one
  admitted edge, one rejected candidate with `edge_capacity`, and different
  final graph fingerprints.
- Near-boundary scores were `0.5856620185738528` and
  `0.5856620185738527`; deterministic tie order remained `left,right`.
- Explicit exact-tie control gave scores `1.0` and `1.0` and selected `left`
  under the declared identifier tie rule.
- Score-shuffled control preserved the raw scores/ranks but selected `right`,
  changing the structural choice without changing opportunity or capacity.
- Reversed and time-shuffled controls produced no ordered local candidates.
- Fixed topology admitted no edge. Uniform intervals and neutral/zero decay
  removed the predicted decay distinction and selected `right`.
- Random selection selected `right` in the declared seed-0 control.
- Mirroring preserved the semantic temporal winner: the selected identifier
  changed to `right`, while its role was `left`. Relabeling selected
  `z_candidate` for the evidence-bearing role rather than identifier-sorted
  `a_candidate`.
- Every run completed. Representative execution counters were configured
  budget `12`, processed events `7`, pending events `0`, peak queue occupancy
  `4`, termination `completed`, and `budget_exhausted = false`.
